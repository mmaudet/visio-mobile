Visio Mobile is a native video conferencing client built on the LiveKit SDK. Join meeting rooms directly from your iPhone or iPad, no browser needed.

Features:
- Real-time audio and video calls
- In-meeting chat
- Participant list
- Room link sharing
- OIDC authentication
- Room creation (public, trusted, restricted)

Visio Mobile is free and open-source software developed by LINAGORA. Compatible with any LiveKit server.
