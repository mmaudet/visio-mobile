Visio Mobile is a native, open-source video conferencing client built on the LiveKit SDK. Join meeting rooms directly from your device — no browser needed.

Features:
- Real-time audio and video calls with up to dozens of participants
- Paginated video grid with swipeable pages
- Speaker mode with dominant speaker and thumbnail strip
- Smart video subscriptions for bandwidth optimization
- End-to-end encrypted chat messages (AES-256-GCM)
- Screen sharing display with focus layout
- Background blur and image filters
- Pre-join lobby with camera preview and audio settings
- Calendar integration (iCal) with upcoming meetings
- OIDC authentication with runtime feature flags
- Room creation (public, trusted, restricted)
- Animated reactions via data channels
- Bluetooth audio routing with reliable device switching
- Adaptive context modes (office, pedestrian, car)
- Full localization in 6 languages

Visio Mobile is free and open-source software (AGPL-3.0) developed by LINAGORA. Compatible with any LiveKit-based server.
